Frio AI monitoring

Catch small problems
before they shut the pad down

Every Frio unit is watched around the clock. Live telemetry streams oil pressure, coolant temperature, battery voltage, RPM, load, and fault codes back to our ops team—so early warning signs get fixed on a planned call, not after a hard shutdown kills production.

  • ✓Predictive fault detection — AI flags trending oil pressure, creeping coolant temps, and voltage drift hours before a critical trip.
  • ✓Managed shutdowns — We schedule a controlled swap instead of waiting for a 2 a.m. catastrophic failure.
  • ✓Fewer emergency callouts — Techs roll with context. Your pad keeps producing.
  • ✓Live fleet visibility — Same eyes we use in the field, available anytime in Fleet Monitor.
